Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

Introduction

PromptSmith ("the Extension", "we", "our", or "us") is a Chrome extension that helps users enhance and improve text using third-party AI providers selected by the user.

Information We Collect

PromptSmith does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

  • No user-generated content is collected or stored.
  • No drafts, prompts, suggestions, emails, messages, or documents are stored by us.
  • No user accounts are required.
  • No analytics are collected regarding what users write.
  • No browsing history is stored.

API Keys

Users provide their own API keys for supported AI providers. These API keys are stored locally on the user's device and are never transmitted to our servers.

  • Stored locally only.
  • Encrypted where supported by browser storage mechanisms.
  • Controlled entirely by the user.

Third-Party AI Providers

When users submit text for processing, that text is sent directly to the AI provider they choose.

Supported providers may include:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy

Users are responsible for reviewing the privacy practices of their selected provider.

Chrome Permissions

storage

Used to save user preferences, settings, and API keys locally on the device.

activeTab

Used to access the current page and detect editable text areas where PromptSmith can assist.

scripting

Used to inject the PromptSmith interface into supported websites.

Data Security

PromptSmith does not operate servers that store user content. User data remains on the device except when voluntarily submitted to a selected AI provider.

Children's Privacy

PromptSmith is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
